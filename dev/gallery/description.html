For journals that publish groups of commissioned articles together: special issues, thematic sections, review symposia, forums. Editors plan such a dossier in OJS instead of spreadsheets and email threads:

  • dossiers with a type, title and description, target issue and section, submission deadline and one or more responsible editors;
  • planned contributions with a working title and a contributor, chosen from the journal's users or created ad hoc, and optional individual deadlines;
  • an editable invitation email with the dossier, the planned contributions, the guidelines, the deadline and a personal submission link;
  • submissions started from that link are ordinary OJS submissions, attached to their contribution, put into the target section (also an editors-only one) and assigned to the target issue; the responsible editors are assigned to them;
  • automatic reminders before and after the deadline, with a configurable maximum;
  • notices to the responsible editors, immediately or as a daily summary, and a history of every step and email;
  • a "Dossier editor" role, e.g. for guest editors, and email templates in English and German.
